Get help / Pedir ajuda

Open a support request / Abrir solicitação de suporte

Include your app version, iOS version, vehicle/head-unit model and steps to reproduce the problem. This is a public support forum: never include passwords, private playlist links, purchase receipts, addresses or personal location.

Purchases / Compras

In the app, open Settings → Car Video Play Pro → Restore purchases. Purchases use your Apple Account. A 24-hour pass runs continuously from its original purchase, including when the app is closed. Restoring does not restart it. Weekly subscriptions renew automatically until canceled in your Apple subscription settings.

Playback / Reprodução

The app includes no paid channels or streaming subscriptions. Add media you own or are authorized to access. Providers may block embedded browsers, external screens or screen sharing. The app does not remove these restrictions. A CarPlay connection alone does not guarantee video compatibility.

Para restaurar compras, abra Ajustes → Car Video Play Pro → Restaurar compras. O passe dura 24 horas corridas desde a compra. Para problemas de reprodução, confira sua fonte e a compatibilidade do serviço. Não envie senhas ou URLs privadas no suporte público.
