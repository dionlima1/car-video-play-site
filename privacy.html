Privacy / Privacidade

Updated / Atualizada: 15 October 2026

Scope

This policy covers Car Video Play, developed by Dion Lima Da Silva. There is no Car Video Play account requirement. Website accounts belong to their respective providers.

Data on your device

Your media library, playlist URLs, shortcuts, settings and browser cookies are stored on your device. Some access information is stored in the iOS Keychain. We do not receive your website passwords, browsing history or videos. You can remove sources and clear website data in Settings. Diagnostic logs, when enabled for troubleshooting, are local unless you choose to share them.

Maps and location

With your permission, location is used to show your position and provide navigation. Map and route requests are sent to Apple services as necessary. We do not operate a location-history service. You can change location permission in iOS Settings. Apple processes data according to its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an anonymous app identifier for purchase validation, restoring access and aggregate purchase analytics. We do not provide your name, email, browsing history, location or video content to RevenueCat. We do not receive card details. See RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

External sites and content

Websites and media servers you open receive the requests needed to load their content, such as your IP address, and apply their own privacy policies. Logging into a provider happens directly with that provider. Screen sharing requires iOS permission; this feature does not upload the screen to our server. Protected content may not be shareable.

Advertising

This store version does not request advertising inventory, use an advertising identifier or track you across other apps for advertising.

Controls and contact

Remove playlists or clear browser data in the app; manage permissions in iOS Settings. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. Use our support page for privacy questions; do not put personal information in public support posts.

Resumo em português

Biblioteca, atalhos, cookies e configurações ficam no aparelho. Não recebemos suas senhas, histórico de navegação ou vídeos. Com sua permissão, a localização permite mapas e navegação pela Apple. Apple e RevenueCat processam compras; o RevenueCat usa um identificador anônimo, sem receber nome, e-mail, localização ou sites visitados. Sites acessados têm políticas próprias. Esta versão da loja não exibe anúncios nem usa identificador de publicidade. Você pode apagar dados do navegador e fontes no app e alterar permissões no iOS.
